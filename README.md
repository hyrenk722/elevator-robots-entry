One line of awkward small talk between two robots sharing a lift: neither of
them pressed a floor, both of them know it, and neither of them is willing to
be the one who says so out loud. The joke lands on the shared, unspoken
embarrassment — two polite machines riding nowhere together — instead of a pun,
and it stays tight at 101 characters on a single line.

> "I checked: neither of us pressed a floor. We're just two robots riding nowhere, too polite to say it."

`main.py` validates the line (1–200 characters, not only whitespace, a single
line, valid UTF-8) and writes it to `/output/entry.txt` (or
`$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt` when that variable is set).
